About Bhuveda GreenTech

Leading the Way in Sustainable Environmental Solutions

Who We Are

Bhuveda Greentech Pvt. Ltd. is a multidisciplinary environmental and geoscience consultancy committed to advancing sustainable solutions for water, land, and environmental management. With a team of experienced hydrogeologists, environmental scientists, GIS experts, and engineers, we provide scientific, data-driven, and field-validated insights to support informed decision making for industries, governments, and communities.

We combine cutting-edge technology with environmental ethics to deliver integrated solutions for resource conservation, climate resilience, and disaster preparedness. Our mission is to create a positive impact on the environment while helping our clients achieve their sustainability goals.
